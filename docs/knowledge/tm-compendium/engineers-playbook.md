# Engineers playbook

Corpus: 2,119 engineers games, 796 wins — 38% win rate, the strongest
faction in Division 1–3 play (field average 25%). Winners' mean final VP:
139.9. All numbers from `tables/engineers.md`.

## Timing signature of winning engineers

Building actions per game (winners vs losers):

| round | builds W/L | upgrades W/L |
|---|---|---|
| setup | 2.0 / 2.0 | — |
| 1 | 1.19 / 1.28 | **3.27 / 2.90** |
| 2 | 1.73 / 1.39 | 0.99 / 0.99 |
| 3 | 2.21 / 1.84 | 1.47 / 1.18 |
| 4 | 2.29 / 1.90 | 1.89 / 1.59 |
| 5 | 2.34 / 1.98 | 2.17 / 2.03 |
| 6 | 2.93 / 2.34 | 2.68 / 2.59 |

Totals: winners build **~12.7 dwellings** and **~12.5 upgrades** per game.
The signature: a heavy round-1 upgrade burst (3.3 upgrades — cheap engineer
TPs/temples immediately), then *build 1.7–2.9 new dwellings every round for
the rest of the game* while continuing to upgrade. Engineers' 1w+1c
dwellings are the cheapest in the game — winning engineers never stop
placing them. A compact final board of 8 buildings is a losing engineers
board; winners sprawl toward a large connected network while upgrading the
core.

## What that implies

- Spend rounds 1–4 turning income into buildings, not into round-tile VP.
  The general playbook's VP curve applies to engineers exactly: winners are
  even with losers on VP until round 3 and win on the rounds 5–6 surge from
  an economy twice the size.
- Feed the dwelling engine with spades continuously (power-action digs,
  dig-track advance early if the spade discount will be used many times).
- Stay adjacent to opponents: discounted TPs (half-price next to a rival)
  and the 83–94% expert leech-acceptance economy are core engineer income.
- Bridges and the stronghold are round 4–6 conversion pieces (3 VP per
  building-connecting bridge at each pass, network glue for the 18/12/6
  final) — they cash out a wide board; they do not substitute for one.
