# General playbook (all factions)

Every claim below is computed from 3,553 four-player Division 1–3 tournament
games (`tables/_overview.md`). "Winners" = shared-rank-1 finishers.

## 1. The game is won in rounds 5–6 — build engine first

Mean VP by end of round, winners vs losers:

| round | winners | losers | gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 23.4 | 23.3 | +0.1 |
| 2 | 28.7 | 28.3 | +0.4 |
| 3 | 40.7 | 38.3 | +2.4 |
| 4 | 59.7 | 54.1 | +5.6 |
| 5 | 85.4 | 76.0 | +9.4 |
| 6 | 153.2 | 131.3 | +21.9 |

Winners are statistically **indistinguishable from losers until round 3**.
Two-thirds of the final margin appears in rounds 5–6 (68 of a 154-point
winning total arrives in the last two rounds). Do not chase early round-tile
VP at the cost of income, buildings, or board position: a round-1..3 action
that buys 2–4 VP instead of compounding economy is how losers stay even
early and lose late.

## 2. Accept leech — power is not optional

Expert accept rates by offer amount (winners and losers behave identically,
so this is consensus, not a winner's luxury):

| amount | offers | accept rate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 111,127 | 94% |
| 2 | 95,351 | 83% |
| 3 | 37,703 | 66% |
| 4 | 14,951 | 42% |
| 5 | 7,318 | 28% |

Rule of thumb: always take 1 (free); take 2 (1 VP) by default; take 3
(2 VP) two times in three — more willingly early; 4+ is usually too dear.
Refusing small leech to "not help yourself fall behind" is not a thing
experts do: 94% and 83% acceptance at amounts 1–2 across 200k+ offers.
Placing dwellings adjacent to opponents creates these offers in both
directions; experts overwhelmingly play adjacent, not isolated.

## 3. Keep pace on actions — the tempo gap is real

Main-track actions per faction per round:

| round | winners | losers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~7.3 | 7.0 |
| 3 | 7.5 | 6.9 |
| 4 | 8.8 | 8.1 |
| 6 | 11.7 | ~10.5 |

Winners take roughly half an action more *every round*, financed by income
and leech power, and the gap widens late. If you are taking 4–5 actions in
a round while opponents take 7+, your engine is underbuilt — convert power,
take power actions, and keep building.

## 4. Build wide, then up

Winners keep building dwellings every single round (they do not stop after
setup): across factions, winners' per-round build counts exceed losers' in
every round of the game, and the biggest single scoring category at the end
(the round-6 surge) is upgrades + towns + final scoring standing on that
wide base. Network final scoring (18/12/6) goes to the three largest
connected groups — in a 4-player game, a compact 5-building cluster
finishes 4th and scores zero.
